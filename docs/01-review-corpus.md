# Prompt 1 — Review corpus: what users say about the top sports apps and sites

Research date: 2 September 2026. Scope: the products an NFL fan actually uses to answer
"when is the game, and where do I watch it?" Reviews were gathered from the Apple App Store,
Google Play, Trustpilot, PissedConsumer, ComplaintsBoard, Android Central and Apple Support
forums, Reddit threads surfaced via search, and trade press that quotes user complaints
(Cord Cutters News, Awful Announcing, Tom's Guide, Athlon, NBC Sports/PFT).

Note on method: this session's network only allowed search-engine access, not direct page
fetches of app-store review pages. Every quote below is taken verbatim from a search result
excerpt of the cited page. Counts and ratings are as reported by the aggregator at the time.

---

## 1. ESPN app (ESPN: Live Sports & Scores)

| PissedConsumer | 1.3K reviews |

## 11. Schedule/reminder utilities: "2025 NFL Schedule & Reminder", SportsCal, MatchSync, 506 Sports

Representative feedback:
- "2025 NFL Schedule & Reminder" (Google Play): "apps promised fewer ads after watching
  advertisements but continued showing ads, leading to uninstalls"; positive: "notified of the
  games/teams they follow at the times they choose"
- Calendar-sync tools (SportsCal, MatchSync): sell "every kickoff lands in your calendar in your
  local timezone" and "flex schedule moves … show up automatically" — i.e., these are the
  known unmet needs they are marketing against. None of them carry channel or coverage data.
- 506 Sports: the de-facto answer to "which game is on in my area", but it is a static image map
  posted "every Wednesday", read by "finding your location on the map, [the] color coding
  device, and interpreting the symbols" — no per-user output, no alerts, no channel numbers.

Sources: [Google Play NFL Schedule & Reminder](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.freemium.android.apps.nfl.calendar), [SportsCal](https://sportscal.io/blog/how-to-sync-nfl-schedule-to-calendar), [MatchSync](https://matchsync.io/sync-nfl-games-calendar), [506 Sports guide](https://usagamesfeed.com/506-sports-guide-to-nfl-college-football-broadcast-maps/), [MapStadiums coverage map](https://www.mapstadiums.com/2025/10/nfl-coverage-map-what-game-is-on-tv-in.html)

## 12. Cross-product fan sentiment on "where is the game"

- Yahoo Sports (2026): "It shouldn't be this hard to figure out which channel is airing an NFL
  game." Fans needed "10 different channels or streaming services" in 2025; cost "at least
  $575, and some spent nearly $800".
- FCC and DOJ opened inquiries in 2025 into sports-streaming fragmentation after fan complaints.
- Broadcaster Tim Brando: fans "get confused where to watch games" as streaming takes over.
- Regional-window rules (CBS/FOX doubleheaders, home-market protection) documented by CBS
  affiliates as a standing source of confusion.
- Pacific/Mountain fans "constantly convert ET times to their local time"; the NFL lists
  everything in ET.
- Pregame vs kickoff: NBC coverage starts 7:00 pm ET for an 8:20 kickoff; FOX NFL Kickoff at
  11:00 and FOX NFL Sunday at noon ET; TNF Tonight at 7:00 pm ET on Prime; the "start time"
  a fan sees is often the show, not the game.

Sources: [Yahoo Sports 2026](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/nfl-fans-continue-annoyed-streaming-230000997.html), [AOL/FCC](https://www.aol.com/articles/nfl-fans-2025-bills-come-174513341.html), [AOL/DOJ](https://www.aol.com/news/nfl-faces-justice-department-probe-151046057.html), [Fox News/Brando](https://www.foxnews.com/sports/broadcaster-tim-brando-suggests-sports-fans-get-confused-where-watch-games-streaming-takes-over.print), [CBS doubleheader rules](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/nfl-rules-for-tv-doubleheader-broadcasts/), [NFL flex procedures](https://www.nfl.com/schedules/flexible-scheduling-procedures), [NBC FNIA](https://www.nbc.com/nbc-insider/football-night-in-america-pre-game-show-what-to-know), [Amazon TNF](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/thursday-night-football-schedule-prime-video), [Fox NFL Sunday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_NFL_Sunday)
