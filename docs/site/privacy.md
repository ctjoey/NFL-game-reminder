# Privacy Policy — Game Time Reminder

**Last updated: 5 September 2026**

Game Time Reminder does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone:

- Your ZIP code, TV market, TV provider, and the streaming services you select
- The teams and games you follow
- Your alert preferences, quiet hours, and any channel numbers you correct

This information is saved in the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent
to us, because there is no account system and no server that belongs to us.
Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your iPhone using iOS notifications. No
notification is sent from a server, and no device token is collected or stored.

## Network activity

The app downloads the public NFL schedule so that game times, networks and schedule
changes stay current. That request contains no personal information and no identifier
tied to you. It is an ordinary request for a public schedule file.

## Calendar

If you choose "Add my games to Calendar," the app writes events to the calendar you
select, using Apple's EventKit. This happens entirely on your device, only after you
grant permission, and only when you tap that button. The app does not read your
existing calendar events.

## Tracking and advertising

There is none. The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, no third-party
trackers, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because nothing is tracked.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone,
including children.
