The game you want is on some channel, and it's never the one you were sure it was. GameDial tells you which.

Give it your ZIP code and what you pay for. From then on, for every game of the season: when coverage starts, when the ball is actually kicked, and the channel where you live. Not "it's on FOX." FOX 8, WJW.

SCORES WHEN YOU OPEN IT
Live scores and the quarter while games are on. Thursday night's final still there on Friday. Records on every matchup — Steelers (2-0) at Patriots (1-1). They never arrive as a notification, and one switch hides them if you're recording.

REMINDERS
When network coverage begins. Thirty minutes before kickoff, an hour, or a day — your call. The night before, when a game is on a service you don't have. The moment a game gets flexed.

YOUR MARKET
Regional games change block by block across this country. GameDial knows which matchup your station is carrying — confirmed once the networks publish the week's map, marked as an estimate until they do. Traveling? Change your market and the channel numbers follow.
