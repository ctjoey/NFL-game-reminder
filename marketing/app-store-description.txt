It's Wednesday night and you're wondering if your team will be on TV this weekend. Or maybe you're traveling and want to know if you'll catch the game from your hotel. GameDial has you covered.

Tell it your ZIP code and how you watch TV, and it shows you every game of the week and exactly where to find it. Not just "it's on FOX." It tells you FOX 8, WJW, the station in your town.

GREEN MEANS YOU CAN WATCH IT
Open the week and the games you can actually watch are in green. That's the ones on your local station, plus the national games on the channels and streaming services you have. Everything else is dimmed, so you're not getting your hopes up for a game your station isn't carrying.

UPDATED EVERY WEDNESDAY
The networks split up the Sunday afternoon games region by region, and the maps come out midweek. GameDial reads them every Wednesday, and your local games turn green on their own. No app update needed.

SCORES AT A GLANCE
Live scores and the quarter while the games are on, final scores the rest of the week, and both teams' records on every game. Recording a game? One switch in Settings hides all of it.

TAP ANY GAME
Tap a game to open the full card: your channel number, your station, when the pregame show starts and every way you can watch it.

REMINDERS THAT HELP
Get a heads-up when pregame coverage starts, or 30 minutes before kickoff, or both. If a game gets moved or flexed to another night, you'll hear about it. And if your team's game isn't on your local station, GameDial tells you, and tells you what's on instead.

ON THE ROAD?
Change your market and everything follows: the games, the stations and the channel numbers. Handy in a hotel room.

Setup takes about a minute. No account, no sign-up, no ads. Your settings stay on your phone.
