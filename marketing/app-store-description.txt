Not sure which channel is actually showing your team? GameDial knows, and it tells you the exact channel in your area.

Enter your ZIP code and pick your TV or streaming service, and you get the game airing on your local stations. It doesn't just say "it's on CBS." It says CBS 4, WFOR — the one in your market.

REMINDERS
Get an alert when network coverage begins, or set it earlier to plan out your day — your call. You'll also know when a game isn't being shown in your area, and the moment one gets flexed.

YOUR MARKET
Regional games change block by block across this country. GameDial knows which matchup your station is carrying, confirmed once the networks publish the week's map.

TRAVELING?
Find out whether your team will be on TV when you get to town. Change your market and the channel numbers follow.

LIVE SCORES
Scores and the quarter while the games are on, finals when they're over, and both teams' records on every matchup. They never arrive as a notification, and one switch hides them if you're recording.

No account. No sign-up. Everything stays on your phone.
