It's five past one on Sunday. You found the remote, and the game you wanted is not on the channel you were sure it was on. Somebody, somewhere, is watching your team. You are watching neither.

GameDial fixes that in about a minute.

Tell it your ZIP code and what you pay for. That's the whole setup. From then on, for every game of the season, it knows when the pregame starts, when the ball is actually kicked, and what channel the game is on where you live. Not "it's on FOX." FOX 8, WJW, in your living room.

NOW IT SHOWS YOU THE SCORES TOO
Open it while the games are on and you see where each one stands and which quarter it's in. Open it Friday and Thursday night's final is right there. Every matchup carries the records — Steelers (2-0) at Patriots (1-1) — so you know what a game is worth before you give it three hours.

Scores never arrive as a notification. They're there when you open the app, and not a second before. Recording something? One switch in Settings hides all of it.

IT REMINDS YOU AT THE RIGHT MOMENTS
• When network coverage begins, so you catch the pregame instead of the second quarter
• Thirty minutes before kickoff, or an hour, or a day — your call
• The night before, when a game is on a service you don't subscribe to
• The moment a game gets flexed to a new day, time or network, with your reminders moved to match
• One weekly rundown of everything you're following, every channel listed

IT KNOWS WHERE YOU ARE
Regional games change block by block across this country. GameDial knows which matchup your local station is carrying, and tells you straight: confirmed once the networks publish the week's map, clearly marked as an estimate until they do. It will never claim a game isn't on your station unless it actually knows.

Traveling? Change your market. Every station, channel number and regional matchup follows you to the hotel. Watch football in a city you've never been to like you grew up there.

AND IT LEAVES YOU ALONE
No account. No sign-up. No trade rumors, no push alerts about somebody's contract. Only the games you chose, only the moments you picked. Everything stays on your phone — there's no server to send it to.

Set it up in a minute, and stop wondering what channel the game is on.
