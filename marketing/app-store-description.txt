It's five past one on Sunday. You found the remote, and the game you wanted is not on the channel you were sure it was on. Somebody, somewhere, is watching your team. You are watching neither.

GameDial fixes that in about a minute.

Give it your ZIP code and what you pay for. From then on it knows, for every game of the season, when coverage starts, when the ball is actually kicked, and what channel the game is on where you live. Not "it's on FOX." FOX 8, WJW, in your living room.

SCORES WHEN YOU OPEN IT
Live scores and the quarter while the games are on. Thursday night's final still there on Friday. Records on every matchup — Steelers (2-0) at Patriots (1-1) — so you know what a game is worth before you give it three hours. They never arrive as a notification, and one switch hides them if you're recording.

REMINDERS YOU ACTUALLY WANT
When network coverage begins. Thirty minutes before kickoff, or an hour, or a day. The night before, when a game is on a service you don't have. The moment a game gets flexed, with your reminders moved to match.

YOUR MARKET, NOT A NATIONAL GUESS
Regional games change block by block across this country. GameDial knows which matchup your station is carrying — confirmed once the networks publish the week's map, clearly marked as an estimate until they do. Traveling? Change your market and every station and channel number follows you.

No account, no sign-up, no trade rumors. Everything stays on your phone.

Stop wondering what channel the game is on.
